Count & Color

Color by number for ages 3 to 7, on iPhone and iPad
What it doesFAQPrivacyTermsContact

Count & Color is a coloring book that teaches numbers. Every picture is split into big areas, each with a number. Your child taps a color on the palette, hears its number, then taps the matching part of the picture. The app says every number and color out loud, so nothing has to be read.

There are no ads, no accounts, no subscription and no tracking. The app works without an internet connection and collects no data.

What it does

Big areas, small fingers.
Every picture comes in three levels. Level 1 has three or four colors and areas a 3-year-old can hit. Level 3 has up to ten colors for 5 to 7-year-olds. A grown-up picks the level. After three clean pictures the app suggests the next one.
Three ways to read the key.
Numbers (1, 2, 3), count the dots (three dots means 3), or shapes (circle, square, triangle) for children who do not know digits yet.
Hints instead of red marks.
Pick the wrong color and the area wobbles, the voice says "This one is a five. Find five", and the right tile lights up. No crosses, no buzzers, no timer, no score.
The picture comes alive.
Finish a picture and it hops, wags or floats, confetti falls, and it goes into the album as a sticker.
Print it for pencils.
Grown-ups can print any picture as an outline with numbers and a color key, so the same page works on paper, and save finished pictures to Photos.
A section for grown-ups.
Tap the gear on the home screen and type the number shown in words. Inside: level and key mode, sound, the digits your child mixes up (such as 6 and 9), printing, saving and the purchase.

Free and Full

Twelve pictures are free, two in each theme, in every mode and at every level, printing included. Count & Color Full is a one-time purchase of $4.99. It unlocks all 60 pictures in six themes (animals, vehicles, sea, dinosaurs, food, seasons), the themes and key modes we add later, and printing for all of them. There is no subscription, and Family Sharing is on.

Questions

My child taps the wrong color again and again.
After each miss the voice names the right number and the tile glows; after three misses the area glows too. Try a lower level in the grown-ups section, or the shapes key for children who do not know digits yet.
Can my child use it alone?
Yes. Every number, color and hint is spoken. Purchases and links are behind the parental gate.
How do I get to the grown-ups section?
Tap the gear on the home screen. You will be asked to type a number written in words, for example "forty-seven", using digits. A new number appears after a wrong answer.
How do I print a picture?
In the grown-ups section, tap the printer next to a picture. A PDF opens in the share sheet with Print, AirDrop and Save to Files. It is a US Letter page with the outline, the numbers and a color key.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open the grown-ups section and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. With Family Sharing, the family organizer must have bought it and Family Sharing must be on for Count & Color.
How do I watch the tutorial again?
Open the grown-ups section and tap "How Count & Color works".
How do I reset progress?
In the grown-ups section tap Reset progress. Deleting the app removes everything as well.
