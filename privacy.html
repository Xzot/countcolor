Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Count & Color does not collect personal information from anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no network requests made by the app.

What stays on your device

Finished pictures, the album, the list of mixed-up digits and settings (level, key mode, sound, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If a grown-up chooses "Save to Photos" for a finished picture, the app asks for permission to add that one image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Children's privacy

Count & Color is made for children aged 3 to 7 and is offered in the Kids Category of the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising.

  • The app never asks for a name, photo, voice recording or location.
  • No third party receives any information from the app. It contains no ads, analytics or other third-party code.
  • Purchases, printing, saving and all links to outside websites are in the grown-ups section behind a parental gate, so a child cannot reach them by accident.
  • To delete your child's progress, use Reset progress in the grown-ups section, or delete the app.

Purchases

Count & Color Full is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase is active.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and message and use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact
